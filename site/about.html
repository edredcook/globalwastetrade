About

Dr Ed Cook

Ed is a plastic pollution and waste management researcher specialising in circular economy in the Global South. He was a core member of the team behind the P2O model and Breaking the Plastic Wave (2020), helped build the waste component of the OECD's Global Plastics Outlook, and co-developed the SPOT model, a global plastic pollution model published in Nature (Cottom et al. 2024). He is lead author of the World Bank's What a Waste 3.0 (2026).
